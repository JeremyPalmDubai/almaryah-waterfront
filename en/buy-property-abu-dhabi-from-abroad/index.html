Buying property in Abu Dhabi from abroad

Four steps. A clear decision.

  1. Choose a residence: request the current unit price, complete floor plan, area, view and furnishing specification.
  2. Review the contract: confirm project registration, payment dates, fees, handover terms and the verified payment beneficiary.
  3. Prepare your documents: passport, identity checks and source-of-funds evidence as required; confirm remote signing or power-of-attorney arrangements.
  4. Plan the transfer and ownership: compare your bank’s exchange rate, allow for fees and seek advice on home-country reporting, tax and inheritance.

A developer instalment plan is not a bank mortgage; salary evidence may not be requested, but compliance checks still apply.

Investor residency & Golden Visa

Abu Dhabi’s property-investor Golden Visa route lists a minimum investment of AED 2 million and can provide a 10-year residence, subject to approval. For off-plan property, ADRO lists an agreement with an approved developer and evidence of at least AED 2 million paid. A 10% booking payment on this starting price is below that paid-amount threshold. Eligibility and timing must be checked for your case with the competent authority.

Payment plan

One-bedroom residences start from AED 4,500,000, approximately USD 1,225,323. This is a category starting price, not a quote for every unit; other categories are priced on request.
