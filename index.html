FAQ

that matter.

Where are the residences located?

Al Maryah Island brings the capital’s business district, waterfront dining and luxury shopping together. Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi and The Galleria are close by, with Saadiyat’s cultural district across the water.

What is the starting price?

One-bedroom residences start from AED 4,500,000, approximately USD 1,225,323. This is a category starting price, not a quote for every unit; other categories are priced on request.

What is the payment plan?

A developer payment plan that spreads the purchase across booking, construction and handover. No monthly or quarterly payments are assumed.

When is handover and are homes furnished?

The supplied commercial brief targets Q1 2030 and furnished delivery. Timing and the precise furnishing package must be confirmed in the unit’s sale agreement.

Can I buy from abroad with only a passport?

Begin remotely with your passport and a personal consultation. Reservation and signing options depend on the developer and registration process. Identity, source-of-funds checks and additional documents may be required. A developer instalment plan is not a bank mortgage; salary evidence may not be requested, but compliance checks still apply.

Does this purchase qualify for a Golden Visa?

Abu Dhabi’s property-investor Golden Visa route lists a minimum investment of AED 2 million and can provide a 10-year residence, subject to approval. For off-plan property, ADRO lists an agreement with an approved developer and evidence of at least AED 2 million paid. A 10% booking payment on this starting price is below that paid-amount threshold. Eligibility and timing must be checked for your case with the competent authority.

Are returns or capital growth guaranteed?

No. Rental income, resale values and occupancy depend on market conditions, costs and the individual property. Review service charges, transaction costs, currency exposure and your own tax position.
